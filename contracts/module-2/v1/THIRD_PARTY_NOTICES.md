## Formula 1 circuit geometries

The source geometries in `source/f1-circuits-2026.geojson` were extracted from:

- Project: `bacinger/f1-circuits`
- URL: https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits
- Copyright: Copyright (c) 2019-2025 Tomislav Bacinger
- License: MIT

The Never Lift generator projects the geographic coordinates into a local metric plane, uniformly scales each closed path to its published circuit length, resamples it and derives gameplay metadata. The generated files remain approximations intended for a personal game project and are not official Formula 1 or FIA data.
